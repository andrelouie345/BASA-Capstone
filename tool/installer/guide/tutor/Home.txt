====== BASA Tutor Guide ======
For tutors using the **BASA Tutor** app.

===== Start here =====
* [[Checklist|Tutor checklist]]: what to do, in order, through the school year.
* [[Functions|BASA Tutor functions]]: what each part of the app does and how to use it.
* [[Glossary]]: the terms the app uses.

===== Before anything else =====
Your device keeps your work until you send it. Before you lend it to anyone, read [[Functions:Sharing a device and switching users]].

===== Finding things in BASA Tutor =====
The main sections are **Home** (dashboard, reminders, calendar, trends), **Learners**, **Assess** (CRLA and Phil-IRI), **ARAL** and **Sync**. Your account menu (your name, at the top) has your password, server settings, colours, About and Sign out.
