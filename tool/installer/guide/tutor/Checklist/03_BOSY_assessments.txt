====== BOSY assessments ======
===== Event =====
The Beginning of School Year round. Grades 1-3: CRLA. Grades 4-10: Phil-IRI in English, the Group Screening Test (GST) first, then the individual reading //(to confirm with ma)//.

===== Required =====
* The BOSY round is open (**Home → Round calendar**).
* You downloaded the latest data. Assessing then works offline.

===== What to do in BASA Tutor =====
Assess each learner of your sections ([[:Functions:CRLA|CRLA]], [[:Functions:Phil-IRI|Phil-IRI]]). Send your work when you are connected ([[:Functions:Getting and sending data|Send my work]]); your coordinator approves it.

===== Steps =====
[ ] Download the latest data before you start.
[ ] Grades 1-3: open **Assess → CRLA by section**, pick the section, and assess each learner in each language their grade takes.
[ ] Grades 4-10: open **Assess → Group Screening Test** and record each learner's GST score.
[ ] Grades 4-10: open **Assess → Phil-IRI by section** (round BOSY) and run each learner's session. Complete it when done.
[ ] Check **Home → Reminders** for learners you haven't assessed yet.
[ ] When connected, open **Sync → Send my work** and send it.
[ ] Open **Send my work** again later to see whether it was approved. If it was rejected, read the reason, fix it and send again.
