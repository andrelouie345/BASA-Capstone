====== End of the school year ======
===== Event =====
Before the year closes: all your work must be on the server and approved.

===== Required =====
* A connection.

===== What to do in BASA Tutor =====
Work left on your device is not in the school's reports, and it stops anyone else from using your device ([[:Functions:Sharing a device and switching users|Sharing a device and switching users]]).

===== Steps =====
[ ] Open **Sync → Send my work** and send everything that is left.
[ ] Open it again until nothing is waiting for approval and nothing was rejected.
[ ] Tell your coordinator you are done.
