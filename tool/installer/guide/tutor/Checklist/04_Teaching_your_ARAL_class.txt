====== Teaching your ARAL class ======
===== Event =====
Your coordinator gave you an ARAL-Reading class. It runs for 32 sessions, with a check-in after the 16th and the 32nd session.

===== Required =====
* The class is yours in **ARAL → Classes** (download the latest data after your coordinator sets it up).

===== What to do in BASA Tutor =====
Log each session and mark attendance ([[:Functions:ARAL-Reading|Sessions and attendance]]), rate each learner's IRIP target every week ([[:Functions:ARAL-Reading|IRIP]]) and give the check-ins ([[:Functions:ARAL-Reading|Check-ins]]). Send your work regularly.

===== Steps =====
[ ] Download the latest data and open **ARAL → Classes**: check your class and its learners.
[ ] After each session: open **ARAL → Sessions and attendance**, log the session (date, minutes, set-up, lesson) and mark each learner present, absent or excused.
[ ] Each week: open **ARAL → IRIP** for each learner, rate the week's target (Observed, Partially observed, Not observed) and start the next week.
[ ] After the 16th session: give the first check-in (**ARAL → Check-ins**) and enter each learner's answers.
[ ] After the 32nd session: give the second check-in.
[ ] Use **ARAL → Materials** to see the materials recommended for each learner's program.
[ ] Check **Home → Reminders** each week: check-ins due, learners without an IRIP, sessions missing.
[ ] Send your work (**Sync → Send my work**) at least once a week when connected.
