====== Start of the school year ======
===== Event =====
A new school year starts. Your coordinator has imported the learners and assigned you your sections.

===== Required =====
* A connection to download.

===== What to do in BASA Tutor =====
Download the new year's data and check your sections and learners ([[:Functions:Your sections and learners|Your sections and learners]]).

===== Steps =====
[ ] Sign in while connected and open **Sync → Download the latest data**.
[ ] Open **Learners → Sections** and check your sections for the new school year.
[ ] Open **Learners → Learners**, pick each section and check its learners. Tell your coordinator about any learner who is missing or wrong. A learner new to the section is first enrolled in LIS by the class adviser (see //A new learner joins your section// in [[:Functions:Your sections and learners|Your sections and learners]]).
[ ] Open **Home → Round calendar** to see when each assessment round opens.
