====== Your first day with BASA Tutor ======
===== Event =====
You start using BASA Tutor. This is done once on each device.

===== Required =====
* BASA Tutor installed on your device.
* Your email and the temporary password from your coordinator.
* The server address and key from your coordinator.
* An internet connection (or the school LAN): the first sign-in must be online.

===== What to do in BASA Tutor =====
Point the app at the server, sign in, choose your own password and download your data ([[:Functions:Signing in and your account|Signing in and your account]], [[:Functions:Getting and sending data|Getting and sending data]]). After that you can work offline.

===== Steps =====
[ ] Open BASA Tutor. On the sign-in screen, open **Server settings** and enter the server address and key. Save.
[ ] Sign in with your email and temporary password.
[ ] Choose your own password when the app asks. Keep it private.
[ ] Open **Sync → Download the latest data**.
[ ] Open **Learners → Sections**: your sections should be there. If one is missing, ask your coordinator to assign it to you.
