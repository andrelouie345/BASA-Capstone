====== Summer Remediation ======
===== Event =====
Only if your coordinator made you a Summer Remediation (SRP) tutor: your SRP classes after EOSY.

===== Required =====
* You downloaded the latest data after your coordinator set up the SRP classes.

===== What to do in BASA Tutor =====
See your SRP classes, their learners, room and schedule ([[:Functions:Summer Remediation|Summer Remediation]]).

===== Steps =====
[ ] Download the latest data.
[ ] Open **ARAL → Summer Remediation** and check your classes, room and schedule.
