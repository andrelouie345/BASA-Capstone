====== Tutor checklist ======
Work through these pages in order. Tick a box when a step is done: click it, or put the cursor on it and press **F12**. The ticks stay in this guide when BASA Tutor is updated.

Stage 1 is done once. Stages 2 to 8 repeat every school year: untick them at the start of a new year.

===== Every time you work =====
[ ] When connected: **Sync → Download the latest data**.
[ ] Work (offline is fine).
[ ] When connected again: **Sync → Send my work**.
