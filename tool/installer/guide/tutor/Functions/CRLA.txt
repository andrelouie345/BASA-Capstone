====== CRLA ======
The Comprehensive Rapid Literacy Assessment, for **Grades 1 to 3**. Each learner is assessed in the languages their grade takes (Mother Tongue, Filipino, English), each round (BOSY, MOSY, EOSY).

===== Assess a learner =====
1. **Assess → CRLA by section**: pick the section and school year.
2. Start an attempt for the learner: the language (for Mother Tongue, the language name, e.g. Cebuano) and the round.
3. Enter Part 1 (the letter and word tasks).
4. Enter Part 2 (the story: miscues, reading time, comprehension answers). The app works out the words read from the story and the miscues.
5. The reading profile shows after saving (from Low Emerging to Reading at Grade Level).
A retake is a new attempt; the latest attempt of a round counts.

===== Scoresheet export =====
**Assess → Scoresheet export**. Fills the blank DepEd CRLA2 scoresheet of the section's grade with a round's results. You need the blank template file.

===== CRLA thresholds =====
**Assess → CRLA thresholds**. The score bands the app uses (set by your school; you can view them).
