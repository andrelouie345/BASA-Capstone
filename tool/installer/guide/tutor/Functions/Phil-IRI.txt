====== Phil-IRI ======
The Philippine Informal Reading Inventory in **English, for Grades 4 to 10**. Rounds: BOSY, MOSY #1, #2, #3, EOSY.

===== GST (Group Screening Test) =====
**Assess → Group Screening Test**. Record each learner's GST score: the total, or literal, inferential and critical.

===== A learner's session =====
1. **Assess → Phil-IRI by section**: pick the section and round, then start the learner's session.
2. Pick a passage. The app offers only passages the learner hasn't read before.
3. Time the reading: the whole text, one minute, or silent reading.
4. Mark the miscues on the passage's words.
5. Enter the answers to the questions and score the rubric.
6. **Complete** the session (or discard it if it was a mistake).
The app shows the reading's result after each save. Your coordinator finalizes the section's round.

===== Learner results =====
**Assess → Learner results**. A learner's results per round and their adjusted reading level. Levels: Independent, Instructional, Frustration, Struggling, Non-Reader. Comprehension carries up from round to round (a lower result never takes it down).

===== Passages and thresholds =====
**Assess → Passages and questions** and **Assess → Phil-IRI thresholds**: what the app uses (view only).
