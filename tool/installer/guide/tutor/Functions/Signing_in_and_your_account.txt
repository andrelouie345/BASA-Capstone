====== Signing in and your account ======
Your account menu is the button with your name at the top of BASA Tutor.

===== Sign in =====
Choose the server (cloud or school LAN), type your email and password, and sign in. The first sign-in on a device must be online. If the app and the server run different BASA releases, the app says what to update.

===== Sign in offline =====
After you have signed in online and downloaded your data on your device, you can sign in on it without a connection for a number of days (14 unless your school changed it). Downloading and sending need an online sign-in. After too many wrong offline passwords, sign in online again.

===== Change password =====
Account menu → **Change password**.

===== Sign out =====
Account menu → **Sign out**. Your work stays on the device but is **not** sent: use **Sync → Send my work** first.

===== Server settings =====
Account menu → **Server settings** (or on the sign-in screen). Set once, with the values from your coordinator.

===== Colours =====
Account menu → **Colours**: light or dark mode and the app's colours, for this device.

===== About =====
Account menu → **About**: the BASA Tutor version. When your coordinator says BASA was updated, update the app.
