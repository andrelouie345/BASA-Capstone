====== Getting and sending data ======
You work on a copy of your data on your device. You download it when connected and send your work back.

===== Download the latest data =====
**Sync → Download the latest data** (online). Gets your sections, learners, classes and the school's settings. Your unsent work is kept. Do it before every round and when your coordinator changes your classes.

===== Send my work =====
**Sync → Send my work**. Shows what you haven't sent and sends it as **one upload**.
* Your coordinator approves it (unless your school approves uploads automatically).
* While it waits, those records can't be changed. To change one, **withdraw** the upload, fix the record and send again.
* If the same record was changed on the server since your download, the app asks which copy to keep: yours or the server's.
* Open **Send my work** again later to see the decision. Approved records take their final form. A rejected upload shows the coordinator's reason and unlocks your records so you can fix them and send again.
