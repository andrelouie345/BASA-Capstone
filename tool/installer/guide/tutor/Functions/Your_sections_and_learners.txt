====== Your sections and learners ======
===== Sections =====
**Learners → Sections**. The sections your coordinator assigned you. If one is missing, ask your coordinator.

===== Learners =====
**Learners → Learners**. Pick a section to see its learners; search by name or LRN. You can save the section's roster as a CSV file.

You also see the learners of the ARAL classes you teach, whatever their section.

===== A new learner joins your section =====
You can't add learners in BASA Tutor: learners come from the section's school form (SF1, from LIS), which your coordinator imports.
[ ] Ask the section's **class adviser** to enrol the new learner in **LIS**.
[ ] Give your **coordinator** the new learner's details: full name, LRN and section.
[ ] When your coordinator says the learner is in BASA, open **Sync → Download the latest data**. The learner then appears in the section and can be assessed.

===== Schools =====
**Learners → Schools**. Your school's details.
