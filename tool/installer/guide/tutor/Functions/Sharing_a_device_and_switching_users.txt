====== Sharing a device and switching users ======
===== Your device holds your data =====
BASA Tutor keeps a copy of your sections and classes on your device so you can work offline. A device holds **one person's data at a time**.

===== Before you lend your device =====
[ ] Open **Sync → Send my work** and send everything.
[ ] Check that nothing is left to send or waiting to be fixed.
[ ] Sign out.

While anything of yours is unsent, **nobody else can sign in on your device**. This keeps your work safe. Lending is for emergencies: finish your work first.

===== When someone else signs in =====
When another person signs in on your device, the app removes your data from it first. When you get the device back, sign in online and download your data again.

===== "Sign-in refused" =====
"This device holds work of ... that is not on the server yet" means the device's owner still has unsent work. Only the owner can fix it: they sign in on that device and send their work.
