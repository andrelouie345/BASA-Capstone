====== Glossary ======
===== BOSY, MOSY, EOSY =====
Beginning, Middle and End of School Year: the assessment rounds. Phil-IRI has three MOSY rounds (#1, #2, #3); CRLA has one.

===== CRLA =====
Comprehensive Rapid Literacy Assessment. Grades 1 to 3, in Mother Tongue, Filipino and English. Gives a reading profile from Low Emerging to Reading at Grade Level.

===== Phil-IRI =====
Philippine Informal Reading Inventory. In BASA: English, Grades 4 to 10. Gives reading levels (Independent, Instructional, Frustration, Struggling, Non-Reader) and an adjusted reading level.

===== GST =====
Group Screening Test: the Phil-IRI test given to the whole class first.

===== ARAL-Reading =====
The reading part of DepEd's ARAL program: tutoring for learners below grade level, in classes of at most 15, for 32 sessions.

===== Key stages and programs =====
ARAL programs, chosen from the learner's adjusted reading level: Foundational (levels 0-1, when the school uses SHARP; otherwise KS1), KS1 (Key Stage 1), KS2 Basic and Plus, KS3 Basic and Plus. The ARAL KS1-KS3 learning materials are for Grades 7-10.

===== IRIP =====
Individual Reading Intervention Plan: weekly reading targets per learner, rated by the tutor.

===== SHARP =====
The Foundational program's reading materials and lesson log, turned on per school in ARAL settings.

===== SRP =====
Summer Remediation Program: classes after EOSY for learners who still need help.

===== LRN =====
Learner Reference Number: the learner's DepEd number. BASA uses it to tell learners apart.

===== SF1 =====
School Form 1: the DepEd school register of a section's learners. BASA imports it.

===== NLA =====
Numeracy and Literacy Assessment: DepEd's portal where the coordinator enters the school's reading assessment results (from **Reports → NLA portal report**).
