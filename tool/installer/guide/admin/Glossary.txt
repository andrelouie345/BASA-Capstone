====== Glossary ======
===== BOSY, MOSY, EOSY =====
Beginning, Middle and End of School Year: the assessment rounds. Phil-IRI has three MOSY rounds (#1, #2, #3); CRLA has one.

===== CRLA =====
Comprehensive Rapid Literacy Assessment. Grades 1 to 3, in Mother Tongue, Filipino and English. Gives a reading profile from Low Emerging to Reading at Grade Level.

===== Phil-IRI =====
Philippine Informal Reading Inventory. In BASA: English, Grades 4 to 10. Gives reading levels (Independent, Instructional, Frustration, Struggling, Non-Reader) and an adjusted reading level.

===== GST =====
Group Screening Test: the Phil-IRI test given to the whole class first.

===== ARAL-Reading =====
The reading part of DepEd's ARAL program: tutoring for learners below grade level, in classes of at most 15, for 32 sessions.

===== Key stages and programs =====
ARAL programs, chosen from the learner's adjusted reading level: Foundational (levels 0-1, when the school uses SHARP; otherwise KS1), KS1 (Key Stage 1), KS2 Basic and Plus, KS3 Basic and Plus. The ARAL KS1-KS3 learning materials are for Grades 7-10.

===== IRIP =====
Individual Reading Intervention Plan: weekly reading targets per learner, rated by the tutor.

===== SHARP =====
The Foundational program's reading materials and lesson log, turned on per school in ARAL settings.

===== SRP =====
Summer Remediation Program: classes after EOSY for learners who still need help.

===== LRN =====
Learner Reference Number: the learner's DepEd number. BASA uses it to tell learners apart.

===== SF1 =====
School Form 1: the DepEd school register of a section's learners. BASA imports it.

===== Archived, undecided, anonymized =====
An **archived** learner left the school (transferred out or dropped out): no longer on active lists or assessed, but every record is kept and can be restored. An **undecided** learner is still active but missing from their section's last imported SF1, waiting for your decision. An **anonymized** learner is an archived one whose name, LRN, birth date and contact details were cleared for good (only after the archive period; never automatic).

===== NLA =====
Numeracy and Literacy Assessment: DepEd's portal where the coordinator enters the school's reading assessment results (from **Reports → NLA portal report**).

===== Words on the CRLA screens =====
**Attempt**: one CRLA test of one learner, in one language and one round. A retake is a new attempt; the latest attempt of a round counts.

**Part 1 and Part 2**: Part 1 is the letter and word tasks. Part 2 is reading a story aloud, then answering questions about it.

**Task 1, Task 2 low, Task 2 high**: the Part 1 tasks. A Task 1 score of 0 to 6 leads to Task 2 low (rhymes and words); 7 or more leads to Task 2 high (sentences). In English, Task 2 follows a Task 1 score of 1 or more.

**Full, Moderate, Light Refresher, Grade Ready**: the Part 1 result, from the most help needed (Full Refresher) to none (Grade Ready).

**Story number**: Part 2 has two stories for each grade and language, story 1 and story 2. Enter the one the learner read.

**Words read**: the story's number of words minus the miscues.

**Reading profile**: the CRLA result after Part 1 and Part 2: Low Emerging, High Emerging, Developing, Transitioning, or Reading at Grade Level.

===== Words on the Phil-IRI screens =====
**Session**: one learner in one round. It holds the learner's readings, their answers and the rubric.

**Passage**: a text the learner reads, made for one grade. A learner never reads the same passage twice.

**Collection**: a set of passages with their questions and rubric.

**Miscue**: a word the learner reads wrong, leaves out, adds or swaps. A miscue counts against word recognition.

**Self-corrected**: a word the learner first read wrong and then fixed alone. It is written down but not counted as a miscue.

**Word recognition**: the share of the passage's words read correctly, as a percentage.

**Words per minute (wpm)**: how fast the learner reads.

**Comprehension**: the share of questions answered correctly, as a percentage. **Looked back**: the learner looked at the passage again to answer; the answer still counts.

**Independent, Instructional, Frustration, Struggling, Non-Reader**: the reading levels, from reading alone with ease (Independent) to not reading yet (Non-Reader).

**Rubric**: a short list of things the tutor observes while the learner reads, each scored on a small scale.

**Complete, Reopen, Finalize**: the tutor completes a session when every reading is done, and can reopen it to fix something. The coordinator finalizes the round; then completed sessions can no longer change.

**Not assessed**: no reading of the learner's own in that round. It counts as Struggling.

===== Words on the ARAL screens =====
**Roster**: everyone in ARAL this school year, with their program.

**Class**: up to 15 learners with one tutor, for one grade and one program (or mixed programs).

**Waiting for a class**: on the roster, but not in a class yet. The coordinator places them in a class.

**Referral**: a learner a teacher asked to put in ARAL, with the teacher's name and reason.

**Exited**: no longer in ARAL: reads at grade level, transferred out, dropped out, classroom support is enough (KS1), or another reason.

**Present, Absent, Excused**: the attendance marks of a session. Excused sessions do not count in the attendance rate.

**Check-in**: a short test after session 16 and after session 32. **Cut-off**: the score a learner needs to meet it (75% unless the school changed it).

**IRIP cycle, week, strand, target**: an IRIP cycle is 8 weeks. Each week the learner works on one target (a reading skill) in each strand (a part of the plan). **✔ ◑ ✘**: the tutor's weekly rating: observed, partly observed, not observed.

===== Words on the sync screens =====
**Unsent work**: work saved on this device that is not on the server yet.

**Send my work**: sends all your unsent work to the server as one upload.

**Waiting for approval**: an upload the coordinator has not approved yet. Until then you cannot change those records on this device.

**Conflict**: someone changed a record on the server after your last download. **Keep mine** sends your version instead; **Take the server's** removes your change from this device.

**Download the latest data**: copies everything you may see from the server to this device. Your unsent work is kept.

**Device owner**: the last person who downloaded on this device. Only they can send its work.

**Offline sign-in**: the device owner can sign in without a connection for some days after the last online sign-in (14 unless the school changed it).

===== Words on the admin screens =====
**Done threshold**: the share of learners that must be assessed for a round to count as done in progress monitoring (85% unless changed).

**Carried and actual values**: carried = a learner's best Phil-IRI result so far; actual = only that round's own reading. Each list uses one (**Manage → Report settings**).

**Approve uploads automatically**: tutors' uploads are applied as soon as they are sent, without review.

**Archive period**: how long an archived learner waits, after their school year, before they may be anonymized (6 months unless changed).

**Form 2b**: the NLA portal's Phil-IRI table for Junior High School.

**BOSY masterlist**: the learners not grade-level ready at the beginning of the school year.

**Phil-IRI ranking**: the lowest learners of one round by one measure.
