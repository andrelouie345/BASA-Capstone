====== During the year - ARAL sessions ======
===== Event =====
ARAL-Reading runs through the year: sessions (32 in all), attendance, the IRIP each week, and check-ins after the 16th and 32nd session.

===== Who =====
* **ARAL tutors** log their own classes' sessions.
* **Coordinator** monitors, reviews uploads and keeps the roster up to date.

===== Required =====
* Classes with tutors (stage 4).
* Tutors download the latest data, then work offline and upload when connected.

===== What to do in BASA =====
Each class has at most one session per date ([[:Functions:ARAL-Reading|Sessions and attendance]]). Tutors rate each learner's weekly IRIP target ([[:Functions:ARAL-Reading|IRIP]]) and give the check-ins ([[:Functions:ARAL-Reading|Check-ins]]). When new results come in, BASA updates programs and exits learners who reached grade level when you open the roster. Reminders show classes with no session for too long.

===== Steps =====
[ ] Each week, open **Home → Reminders**: classes with no session lately, check-ins due, learners without an IRIP.
[ ] Each week, open **Sync → Review uploads** and decide the tutors' uploads.
[ ] After a class's 16th session, check that its learners took the first check-in (**ARAL → Check-ins**).
[ ] After the 32nd session, check the second check-in.
[ ] Open **ARAL → Roster** after each round of results: BASA updates programs and exits grade-level-ready learners. Read the list it shows.
[ ] Exit learners who transfer or drop out (with the reason and date). Place new referrals in a class.
[ ] Keep the learning materials list current (**ARAL → Materials**): titles, file names and the Google Drive links for your school.
