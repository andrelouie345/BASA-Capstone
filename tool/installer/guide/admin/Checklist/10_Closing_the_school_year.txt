====== Closing the school year ======
===== Event =====
Before the next school year: every device's work is on the server and decided.

===== Who =====
* **Everyone**; the **coordinator** checks.

===== Required =====
* A connection on every device.

===== What to do in BASA =====
Work that stays on a device is not in reports and can block the next user of that device ([[:Functions:Sharing a device and switching users|Sharing a device and switching users]]). Before a BASA update that needs every device to update, this list is required.

===== Steps =====
[ ] Ask every tutor to open **Sync → Send my work** and send what is left.
[ ] Open **Sync → Review uploads** until nothing is pending for your school.
[ ] Ask tutors whose uploads were rejected to fix them and send again.
[ ] Check a few learners in **Manage → Learner history** to see the year is complete.
[ ] Save the year's report files somewhere safe (they are made from the server's data at any time, but keep the submitted copies).
