====== MOSY rounds ======
===== Event =====
The Middle of School Year rounds: CRLA MOSY (one round); Phil-IRI MOSY #1, #2 and #3. Grade 1 learners enter ARAL from their CRLA MOSY result.

===== Who =====
* **Tutors** assess.
* **Coordinator** follows up, reviews uploads, finalizes Phil-IRI, enrolls Grade 1.

===== Required =====
* The round is open in the Round calendar (dates per round //(to confirm with ma)//).
* Tutors downloaded the latest data.

===== What to do in BASA =====
The same work as BOSY ([[:Functions:CRLA|CRLA]], [[:Functions:Phil-IRI|Phil-IRI]]) for each MOSY round. Phil-IRI uses a different passage each round (a learner never reads the same passage twice). After CRLA MOSY, enroll the Grade 1 learners who qualify ([[:Functions:ARAL-Reading|Roster]]).

===== Steps =====
[ ] Check **Home → Round calendar**: the MOSY round is open.
[ ] Remind tutors to download the latest data before they assess.
[ ] Tutors run CRLA MOSY (Grades 1-3) and the Phil-IRI MOSY round (Grades 4-10).
[ ] Review uploads (**Sync → Review uploads**) every few days.
[ ] Follow **Reports → Progress monitoring** for the round; finalize finished sections (**Assess → Finalize**).
[ ] After CRLA MOSY: open **ARAL → Roster** for each Grade 1 section, enroll the proposed learners and place them in classes.
[ ] Open **ARAL → Roster** for every section so BASA applies the new results (program changes, exits).
[ ] Repeat for MOSY #2 and MOSY #3 of Phil-IRI.
