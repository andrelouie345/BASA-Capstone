====== After BOSY - ARAL enrollment ======
===== Event =====
BOSY results are in. Choose the learners for ARAL-Reading, put them in classes and give each class a tutor.

===== Who =====
* **Coordinator** (or school admin).

===== Required =====
* BOSY results approved and on your device (download the latest data first).
* Grade 1: CRLA MOSY decides ARAL entry, so Grade 1 learners join after MOSY.
* The tutors who will teach ARAL classes have accounts.

===== What to do in BASA =====
BASA proposes the learners who qualify, from each section's results ([[:Functions:ARAL-Reading|Roster]]). You enroll them, and BASA suggests their program from their results. Then you form classes of at most 15 active learners and give each a tutor ([[:Functions:ARAL-Reading|Classes]]). A learner's ARAL tutor is their class's tutor. The BOSY masterlist ([[:Functions:Reports|BOSY masterlist]]) lists the learners who are not grade level ready.

===== Steps =====
[ ] Download the latest data (**Sync → Download the latest data**).
[ ] Open **Reports → BOSY masterlist** and look over the learners who are not grade level ready.
[ ] Open **ARAL → Roster**, choose a section, and look at the learners BASA proposes. Enroll them. Repeat for every section.
[ ] Add teacher referrals if a teacher asks for a learner who does not qualify by results (with who referred and why).
[ ] Check each enrolled learner's program (BASA suggests it from the adjusted reading level). Change it where you know better.
[ ] Open **ARAL → Classes** and create the classes: a name, a home grade, one program or mixed programs, and the tutor.
[ ] Place the enrolled learners in classes (at most 15 active learners per class). A learner of another grade needs your confirmation.
[ ] Open **ARAL → Tutors** and check each tutor's classes and learners.
[ ] Tell the ARAL tutors to download the latest data so their classes appear.
