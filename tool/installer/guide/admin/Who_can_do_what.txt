====== Who can do what ======
===== Superadmin (regional) =====
An admin with no school. Sees and manages every school: adds schools and their admins and coordinators, sets the region's round dates and defaults (thresholds, settings, materials, Phil-IRI content), reviews any school's uploads and makes reports for any school and the region. Uses the **BASA** app.

===== School admin =====
An admin of one school. The same as a coordinator for that school.

===== Coordinator =====
Manages one school: its sections, learners (SF1 import), tutors' accounts and assignments, ARAL roster and classes, the school's dates and settings, uploads and reports. Can also assess. Uses the **BASA** app.

===== Tutor =====
Assesses and teaches: sees only their assigned sections and the learners of their ARAL classes; runs CRLA and Phil-IRI, logs ARAL sessions, attendance, IRIP and check-ins; sends their work for approval. Uses the **BASA Tutor** app, which has its own guide.
