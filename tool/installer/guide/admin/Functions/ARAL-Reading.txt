====== ARAL-Reading ======
The ARAL-Reading program: learners below grade level get tutoring in small classes.

===== Roster =====
**ARAL → Roster**. Who is in ARAL, their program, class and status.
* **Who qualifies**: Grade 1 by their CRLA MOSY result, Grades 2-3 by CRLA BOSY (Low or High Emerging; Developing and Transitioning too when the school turns on //KS1 extended//); Grades 4-10 by their Phil-IRI English result below grade level. When your school sets the //basis round// to MoSY, new learners are chosen from the latest round up to MoSY instead (BoSY when a learner has no MoSY result).
* BASA **proposes** the qualifying learners of a section; you enroll them. A teacher can **refer** a learner (who referred, and why).
* BASA suggests each learner's **program** from their **adjusted reading level**: 0-1 Foundational (only when the school has SHARP on, otherwise KS1), 2-3 KS1, 4-5 KS2 Basic, 6-7 KS2 Plus, 8 KS3 Basic, 9 KS3 Plus. Learners at grade level (Instructional or Independent) are not in ARAL. You can change a program.
* When new results arrive, opening the roster updates programs and **exits** learners who reached grade level. Transfers and drop-outs are exited by hand.
* Enrolling and placing in a class are two steps: an enrolled learner may wait for a class.

===== Tutors =====
**ARAL → Tutors**. Each ARAL tutor's classes and active learners.

Each tutor can have a **tutor type**: //internal// (a teacher of your school) or //external// (from outside). The type shows on the ARAL forms, and your school's daily tutor limits (see //School ARAL rules//) go by it. A tutor without a type has no daily limit.

Each section can have an **adviser**: a typed name, the adviser's BASA account, or both. The name shows on ARAL Form 1. The account is what the adviser rule checks, so link it when your school uses that rule.

===== Classes =====
**ARAL → Classes**. A class has a name, a home grade, one program (or mixed programs) and a tutor. At most **15 active learners** (or your school's maximum, see //School ARAL rules//), from any section of the school. A learner of another grade needs the coordinator's confirmation. A learner's ARAL tutor is their class's tutor.

A new class without a name gets the next free memo-style code, e.g. **ARAL-R-G7-02** (Reading, Grade 7, class 2); you can still type any name. Each class can also record its **session type**: //pull-out// or //before/after class//.

===== Sessions and attendance =====
**ARAL → Sessions and attendance**. The class tutor logs each session (date, minutes, set-up, lesson) and marks attendance: present, absent or excused. One session per class per date. The program has 32 sessions. Your school can allow weekdays only and set the longest session (see //School ARAL rules//).

===== IRIP =====
**ARAL → IRIP**. The Individual Reading Intervention Plan (cycles of 8 weeks unless your school set another length): each week a reading target per learner, rated Observed, Partially observed or Not observed. BASA suggests the next target. For KS2 and KS3 programs.

===== Check-ins =====
**ARAL → Check-ins**. The check-in assessments after the 16th and 32nd session, unless your school set other sessions (KS2 and KS3). The tutor enters the answers; BASA scores them against the cut-off (75% unless your school changed it) as Met or Not met. Retakes are allowed; the latest counts. Admins and coordinators see the check-in report.

===== ARAL settings =====
**ARAL → ARAL settings**. For your school: the check-in cut-off, SHARP (the Foundational program), KS1 extended, and after how many days without a session a class shows in the reminders.

===== School ARAL rules =====
DepEd and the Division change ARAL's numbers from time to time, so they are school settings. A rule you never set works as it always has.
* **Class maximum** (1-50, normally 15): a full class takes no new learner. Lowering it never removes anyone: a class above the new maximum keeps working (sessions, attendance, exits), and your reminders say //Class "X" has 12 active learners, more than the maximum 10: move learners to another class. Their records stay.// Take learners out of the class (**Classes → Unplace**) and place them in another one; their attendance and results stay with them.
* **Class minimum** (0 = none): a class below it shows in your reminders (combine it with the nearest level of the same grade if there is one). It never blocks anything.
* **Weekdays only**: no session on Saturday or Sunday, and no //Weekend// set-up.
* **Longest session** (1-120 minutes): a longer session is refused. Not set: BASA only warns above 60 minutes on a weekday.
* **Sessions a week** (0 = none): from Wednesday, a class with fewer sessions this week shows in the tutor's and your reminders.
* **Basis round** (BoSY or MoSY): the round new ARAL learners are chosen from (see //Roster//).
* **Check-in sessions** (normally after sessions 16 and 32).
* **IRIP weeks** (1-26, normally 8): a cycle already longer keeps its weeks.
A school following **Division Memorandum 180 s. 2026** (Valencia City) sets: class maximum 10, class minimum 5, weekdays only on, longest session 60, sessions a week 3, basis round MoSY, and turns **KS1 extended** on (the memo takes Developing and Transitioning learners in Grades 1-3 too). The memo also says a school short of tutors must not enlarge its classes: keep the maximum at 10 and serve the learners with the lowest results first.

Three more rules, off unless your school sets them:
* **Advisers tutor their own learners** (normally yes). Turned off: a learner can't be placed in a class whose tutor is their section's adviser, and an adviser can't become the tutor of a class that holds their own learners. A learner who is refused waits for another class. Changing a section's adviser later never removes anyone from a class.
* **Internal tutor: sessions a day** (1-10): an internal tutor's next session that day is refused once they reach it, across all their classes.
* **External tutor: minutes a day** (1-600): an external tutor's session is refused when it would take them past it that day.
A tutor's type changed later never re-checks their past sessions.

===== Materials and recommendations =====
**ARAL → Materials**. The learning materials list: title, file name and a Google Drive link (BASA stores no files). BASA recommends materials for each learner's program. A school can set its own link for any material.

===== Readability log (SHARP) =====
**ARAL → Readability log**. Only when SHARP is on: the readability (Flesch reading ease and grade) of the texts used.

===== ARAL Forms 1, 2 and 4 =====
**Reports → ARAL forms** (admin build). The Division Memorandum 180 s. 2026 forms for Reading, saved as Excel files from this device's copy (download the latest data first):
* **Form 1**, the class list: one sheet per class, with the school, class code, grade, tutor and type, session type, days and time, venue, number of learners, and each learner's LRN, name, sex, section, adviser and MoSY tool and result.
* **Form 2**, the classes: one row per class, its MoSY result (the one all its learners share, else //mixed//), tutor, type, the tutor's advisory class, schedule and venue, and the number of classes, learners and tutors.
* **Form 4**, the progress report, as of a date: per grade, learners identified at BoSY, served (marked present at least once), reached grade level, improved but still below, no improvement, and in ARAL at MoSY; then classes, tutors, sessions held and hours.
What BASA doesn't hold is left blank for you to fill in by hand (district, workbook issued, remarks, Mathematics, signatures), and each form says so at the bottom. A learner without a MoSY result of their own has a blank MoSY result on Form 1 and counts under //No improvement// on Form 4, named at the bottom. So does a learner with a MoSY result but no BoSY result (for example Grade 1, whose CRLA starts at MoSY): there is nothing to compare, and the bottom of the form names them.

**Forms 3, 5 and 6** are not in BASA: tutor details, printing and overload pay are kept in the separate tutor-management system, so BASA leaves them out. BASA's session records give each tutor's ARAL hours for them.

===== Implementation report and roster files =====
**Reports → ARAL implementation report** (admin build). The report per section and grade, saved in the DepEd template (you need the blank file) or as CSV. The roster can also be saved or imported as a file.
