====== Users and tutor assignments ======
===== Users =====
**Manage → Users**. The accounts you may manage: the superadmin manages everyone; a school admin or coordinator manages their own school's. A tutor's row has a //Tutor type// button: //internal// (a teacher of your school) or //external// (from outside). It shows on ARAL Forms 1 and 2 and decides which daily ARAL limit applies (see //ARAL-Reading → School ARAL rules//).
* **Create** an account: email, full name, role (admin, coordinator or tutor) and school. BASA shows a **temporary password once**: write it down and give it to the person privately. They choose their own password at the first sign-in.
* **Turn off** an account when someone leaves (the switch on its row; the list shows it as **Off**): they can no longer sign in online, and a device they used stops their offline sign-in the next time it reaches the server for them. Turn it on again to restore it.

===== Sign-in names (emails) =====
BASA never sends mail: the "email" is only the sign-in name. Agree one pattern in your division and use it for every account, for example **surname.firstname@schoolname.com**. Two people with the same name at one school: add a number (**delacruz.juan2@schoolname.com**).
A sign-in name belongs to **one account for good**, even after the account is turned off. BASA refuses a second account with the same name on the same server.

===== When a tutor leaves =====
1. **Turn off their account** (above). They can no longer download data or send work.
2. **Get the school's device back** if they used one. Until then they can still sign in offline on it, for up to the offline sign-in window (14 days unless your school changed it), and read what is on it. They can't send or download anything. When the next tutor signs in online on that device, BASA clears the old tutor's copy first.
3. **Give their classes another tutor**: ARAL → Classes → the class → **Change the tutor**, and the tutor of their Summer Remediation classes. Until then a session you log for those classes must name another tutor of your school.
4. **Remove their sections** in Tutor assignments, so the list stays clean.
5. **Their past work stays**: sessions, attendance, assessments and check-ins keep their name.
**Moving to another school in the region:** turn off the old account, and the new school creates a **new account with that school's sign-in name** (e.g. **surname.firstname@newschool.com**). The old name can't be reused, and the old school's records stay under the old account.

===== Tutor assignments =====
**Manage → Tutor assignments**. Which sections each tutor assesses. A tutor can have many sections and a section many tutors. A tutor sees only their sections' learners, plus the learners of the ARAL classes they teach. After a change, tutors download the latest data.

===== Login logs =====
**Manage → Login logs**. Recent sign-in attempts, from this device and the server: who, when, and why a failed one failed. Useful when someone can't sign in.

===== First admin (new server) =====
The first superadmin of a new server is created by the person who sets up the server. You only need this when a new BASA server is installed.
