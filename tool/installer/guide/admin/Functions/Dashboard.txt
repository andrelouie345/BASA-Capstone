====== Dashboard ======
===== Dashboard home =====
**Home → Dashboard**. The year's figures for your role: a tutor sees their own work, a coordinator their school, the superadmin every school with regional totals. Pick the school year at the top.

===== Round calendar =====
**Home → Round calendar**. Each round's dates for CRLA and Phil-IRI, and whether it is upcoming, open or closed. The superadmin sets the region's dates; a school can change a round's dates or skip it. A round not in the calendar shows as //not scheduled// in the reports.

===== Reminders =====
**Home → Reminders**. What needs attention: learners not assessed in an open or closed round, check-ins due, learners without an IRIP, classes with no session lately.

===== Trends =====
**Home → Trends**. Charts by school year: Phil-IRI levels and completion, CRLA profiles, ARAL exits and activity (sessions and attendance). Filter by section, school or region.
