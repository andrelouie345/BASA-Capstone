====== Sharing a device and switching users ======
Read this before you lend your device or borrow someone else's.

===== Why a device holds one user's data =====
When you download the latest data, BASA copies everything //you// may see onto the device: an admin's copy can hold every school. So that nobody sees data that isn't theirs, a device holds **one person's data at a time**: the person who last downloaded on it.

===== Before you lend the device =====
[ ] Open **Sync → Send my work** and send everything.
[ ] Wait until it is approved (or ask the coordinator to approve it), or check that nothing is left to send.
[ ] Sign out.

If anything of yours is still unsent, BASA will not let anyone else sign in on this device. This is on purpose: your work stays safe, and lending is for emergencies only. **It is the lender's job to finish their work before lending the device.**

===== Signing in as someone else =====
When another person signs in online on a device, BASA removes the previous person's data from it first and tells them so. They then download their own data (**Sync → Download the latest data**) before starting.

The previous owner can no longer sign in offline on that device. When they get it back, they sign in online and download their data again.

===== When sign-in is refused =====
"Sign-in refused: this device holds work of ... that is not on the server yet" means the device's owner has unsent work. There is no way around it: the owner signs in on that device and sends their work (**Sync → Send my work**). Then the other person can sign in.
