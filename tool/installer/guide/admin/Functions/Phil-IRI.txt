====== Phil-IRI ======
The Philippine Informal Reading Inventory in **English, for Grades 4 to 10**. Rounds: BOSY, MOSY #1, MOSY #2, MOSY #3, EOSY.

===== GST (Group Screening Test) =====
**Assess → Group Screening Test**. Record each learner's GST score (the total, or literal, inferential and critical). BASA shows who passed. For a learner with only a GST at BOSY, BASA gives a starting grade from the GST as a reference; the individual reading confirms it.

===== Section Phil-IRI sessions =====
**Assess → Phil-IRI by section**. Pick a section and round: each learner's session. Start a session for a learner from here.

===== Assessment session =====
One learner, one round:
1. Pick a passage. BASA offers only passages the learner hasn't read in an earlier round.
2. Time the reading: the whole text, one minute, or silent reading.
3. Mark the miscues on the passage's words.
4. Enter the answers to the questions and score the rubric.
5. Complete the session (or discard it).
BASA shows the reading's result after each save.

===== Learner results =====
**Assess → Learner results**. A learner's results per round: word recognition, comprehension, the word reading profile, the reading profile and the adjusted reading level. The levels are Independent, Instructional, Frustration, Struggling and Non-Reader.

Comprehension **carries up** from round to round (GST, BOSY, MOSY #1-3, EOSY): a better result counts, a lower one never takes it down. A round not given keeps the last result. Word reading can go down.

===== Finalize =====
**Assess → Finalize** (admin build). Close a section's round when its sessions are done.

===== Content (collections, passages, questions, rubric) =====
**Assess → Passages and questions**. The passage sets BASA uses. Everyone can read them; admins and coordinators edit their school's, the superadmin the region's.

===== Phil-IRI thresholds =====
**Assess → Phil-IRI thresholds**. The percentage bands for word recognition and comprehension. The defaults follow DepEd; a school can change them.
