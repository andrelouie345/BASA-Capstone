====== Getting and sending data ======
BASA works offline. You download a copy of your data, work on it without a connection, and send your work when you are connected again.

===== Download the latest data =====
**Sync → Download the latest data** (needs an online sign-in). Gets everything you may see from the server. Your unsent work on the device is kept. Do it at the start of every round, and after a coordinator changes classes, assignments or settings.

===== Send my work (upload) =====
**Sync → Send my work**. Shows what is unsent and sends it as **one upload**.
* A tutor's upload waits for a coordinator's approval, unless the school approves uploads automatically.
* An admin's or coordinator's own upload is applied at once.
* While an upload waits, its records can't be changed on the device. To change one, withdraw the upload, fix it, and send again.
* If someone changed the same record on the server since your download, BASA asks which copy to keep (yours or the server's) before sending.
* After a decision, open **Send my work** again: approved records take their final form; a rejected upload unlocks them so you can fix and resend.

===== Undo work you haven't sent =====
**Sync → Download the latest data → ⋮ (More) on "This device" → Delete unsent work and download**. Deletes **all** of this device's work that is not on the server yet, then downloads the latest data. Use it to undo a mistake you haven't sent, for example a wrong Phil-IRI import (the imported results and the section's workbook lock) or a trial run. An SF1 import is not unsent work: it goes to the server at once, so this doesn't remove it.
* It deletes everything unsent, not only the mistake. **Tip:** send your other work first, then do the risky step (an import, a trial). If it goes wrong, only that step is deleted.
* It can't be undone, and it can't undo what you already sent: an admin's or coordinator's upload is applied at once.

===== Review uploads =====
**Sync → Review uploads** (admin build). Lists your school's uploads waiting for a decision (the superadmin sees every school). Open one to see what it holds, then **approve** it or **reject** it with a reason the tutor will read. Approving does not change your own device: download the latest data to see the result.

===== Approve uploads automatically =====
**Manage → Report settings** → approve uploads automatically. When on, tutors' uploads for your school are applied without review. Turn it on only if you trust the entries without checking them.
