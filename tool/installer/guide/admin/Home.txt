====== BASA Guide ======
For reading coordinators, school admins and the regional superadmin, using the **BASA** app.

===== Start here =====
* [[Checklist|School-year checklist]]: what to do, in order, from setup to the end of the school year.
* [[Functions|BASA functions]]: what each part of BASA does and how to use it.
* [[Who can do what]]: superadmin, school admin, coordinator and tutor.
* [[Glossary]]: the terms BASA uses.
* [[Functions:Importing data|Importing data]]: moving Phil-IRI results from the VNHS ARAL workbook or a scoresheet file into BASA.

===== Before anything else =====
If you share this device with someone, read [[Functions:Sharing a device and switching users]] first.

===== Finding things in BASA =====
BASA's main sections are **Home** (dashboard, reminders, calendar, trends), **Learners**, **Assess** (CRLA and Phil-IRI), **ARAL**, **Sync**, **Manage** and **Reports**. Your account menu (your name, at the top) has your password, server settings, colours, About and Sign out.
